Keyboard and either joystick port are available together. At the menu, Space, M or either button confirms the difficulty. Control and Enter also advance past the opening illustration and title, although neither screen shows a prompt.

Release fire and press it again for another jump. To leave pause, release the keys and press a gameplay key. \key{V} switches music on or off.
